Persona Responsibilities
🎧
L1 Support
First point of contact. Account queries, document requests, basic transfers, online banking help. Resolves simple issues; escalates complex ones.
🔍
L2 Support
Receives escalations from L1. Handles trade finance queries, FX disputes, complex KYC, credit facility queries. Escalates decisions requiring approval.
📊
Middle Office
Risk, credit, compliance. Credit approval, AML/EDD, product structuring, syndication, regulatory reporting. Approves and passes to Back Office.
⚙️
Back Office
Operations and processing. LC issuance, SWIFT execution, trade settlement, nostro reconciliation, documentation. Terminal resolution point.
